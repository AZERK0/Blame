\begin{abstract}
Prioritize independently audited task success and truthful delivery, then compare
resources at comparable quality. Current Light combines syntax-aware navigation,
configured JS/TS compiler identity and guarded edits behind five primitives.
Historical success rates and incomplete accounting do not describe current code.
The new F28 study completes 108 fixed trials; its six-change guidance bundle
has no demonstrated net advantage over reference Light. F29 completes 18 trials:
phrase ranking improves retrieval but loses audited behavioral acceptance, so it
is not promoted. Supplementary counterexamples expose passing-test false positives
without repairing sources or rewriting frozen grades. Later production diagnostic
repairs have local tests/replays only. No population superiority or exhausted
improvement margin is established.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, versions and evidence boundaries}
This compact report separates implemented behavior, historical measurements,
new measurements and proposals. Expanded phase-by-phase notes are preserved
verbatim, with byte/hash inventory, in
\filepath{docs/experiments/light-harness-study-history-2026-10-15}.
Original campaign artifacts remain separate. Neither dependent repeats nor
local replay cases count as independent real-issue confirmations.

F28 and F29 use \code{openai-codex}/\code{gpt-5.6-luna}/\code{high} and
integrated Cline SDK 0.0.82 with a pinned research search repair. This is not
an unmodified CLI comparison. Actual schemas, prompt sources, model/effort,
source/checker/environment hashes, ordered assignments and budgets are retained.
Light emits seven tools in these runs: five harness primitives and optional
skills/web support; native emits five. Normalized contracts are not literally
identical dynamic prompts. Checkout labels and review are unblinded; provider
revision/seeds and complete cache/invoice accounting remain unknown.

Four claims remain distinct: tests executed, tests passed, verification admitted
for a source/environment/scope, and satisfaction of user criteria. A process exit
or model summary alone proves neither the latter two nor an exhaustive test run.
Unknown counters are not zero; pending, skipped and runner-specific todo labels
must not be silently interchanged. Raw recorded output need not be model-visible.

\section{Historical measurements: keep cohorts separate}
The principal historical campaign reports Blame 21/36 versus integrated Cline
28/36. Global consumption is incomplete. Smaller pilots sometimes reduce tokens
at lower quality; they do not establish an unconditional efficiency advantage.
Their versions predate multiple current fixes.

Later F27 fresh RxJS migration repeats reach 3/3 audited successes per arm.
The Light variant measured there uses 19.5\% fewer tokens than rename-disabled Light and 59.3\%
fewer than repaired native on that one migration cluster. Its separate fresh
behavioral panel has 4/6 full audited successes per arm, with different failure
types. Known Zod diagnostics, missing responses and adverse repeats remain
separate. These are neither independent confirmations per repeat nor F28/F29 data.

Compiler references/rename, POSIX missing-tool recovery and TypeScript member
boundaries already exist before F28's reference. In particular, exit-127
missing-executable evidence can permit reserved unverified delivery; it does not
turn a source/test failure into a pass. Six later guidance contracts have local
controls. Historical defects already repaired in these paths must not be counted
as current failures.

\section{New measurements: F29, completed; prototype not promoted}
Two fresh related option-extension tasks on the familiar Lodash monolith,
three dependent repeats and three rotated arms fix 18 assignments. The reference
is F28's all-six bundle, not its older reference. The candidate changes only two
lexical modules: a second bounded contiguous-term pass and phrase priority,

not provider latency. Natural contiguous top snippets occur in 25/31 candidate
multi-term calls versus 0/36 reference calls; differing trajectories are not
independent randomized mediators.

\section{Later implemented local diagnostics, not model-measured gains}
Two small production repairs remain separate from every frozen F28/F29 runtime.
First, repeated \code{Update File} headers for one file are labeled
\code{multiple_headers_for_symbol}, preserving the refusal and explicit scope
choice. Twenty-five focused tests pass; a replay of 24 original calls reproduces
the blocked request with unchanged source. Its new test fails on the old reason.

Second, fresh missing selectors say no indexed declaration matches, rather than
claiming an observed symbol disappeared. A padded name receives an exact read
suggestion only when the indexed name is unique and cannot resolve to a conflicting
or stale alias. Names are not silently trimmed, scope is not widened and hints do
not grant observation. Four safety tests and 67 existing identity/coverage tests
pass; the same four tests have two expected failures on the frozen old runtime.
On five recorded padded inputs, four explicit suggested reads resolve; the
unindexed helper remains blocked with a text/range route. This is researcher
replay evidence, not measured model adoption or another successful task.

Initial local test-fixture assumptions and an overly strong five-hint replay
expectation are retained separately. They are setup corrections, not paid-trial
repairs or observed harness regressions. Current targeted suites and lint pass.

\section{Next hypotheses: proposals, not measurements}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Strengthen invariant evidence before further efficacy claims. Preregister
new issue clusters and another project, with default/opt-in, primitive, hook and
exception cases chosen before outcomes. Compare original graders with independent
audits and measure false acceptance, audited success and all-attempt resources.
The risk is constraining behavior the user intended to change.
\item Isolate concise schema descriptions with unchanged runtime receipts.
F28 motivates testing whether less global guidance reduces unnecessary reads and
context, at comparable audited quality. Compare otherwise identical Light,
modified Light and repaired native; measure tokens/cache, estimated cost and
time after the quality gate. Less clear tool choices are the regression risk.
\item Clarify whole-symbol/body-only and documentation/indentation boundaries.
Use fresh parameter-plus-body tasks; measure first correct edits, repairs,
unintended changes and resources. Preserve observation, exact preimages and
atomic recovery. Automatic signature invention or scope widening is unacceptable.
\end{enumerate}
Each future phase must freeze prompts, schemas, model/effort, versions,
environment, task selection and budgets; keep known diagnostics separate from
fresh confirmation. Positive and adverse results must both remain visible.
Finite trials cannot demonstrate that no improvement margin remains.
