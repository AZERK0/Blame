**Decision: retain T2 as a narrow contract-comprehension improvement; do not claim a global quality or efficiency gain.** Invalid-kind attempts disappear separately in canary, global and fresh confirmation, all original execution/coverage guards remain, and registered completed quality does not decrease. The global predicted reduction in total editing calls fails (72 to 75), reference token point estimate is adverse, and a new semantic counterexample removes the small primary quality advantage. Some alternate post-hoc conventions even favor reference; statistical noninferiority and causal quality improvement are unproved. These unfavorable observations limit retention to clearer operation grammar. Do not pool campaigns or use confirmation to replace the global result. T1 remains the previously demonstrated conditional global token reduction; these new measurements neither remeasure nor cancel its separate evidence.
